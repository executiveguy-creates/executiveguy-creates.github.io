Privacy Policy

Lotto Check · Effective [5-26-2026]

Lotto Check is an offline iOS utility for checking Pick 3, Pick 4, and Pick 5 lottery tickets you have already played. We take privacy seriously and have designed the app to collect nothing.

Information We Collect

None. Lotto Check does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics, or the numbers you enter into the app.

How Your Data Is Handled

Winning draws and played tickets you enter are processed entirely on your device. They are not saved between sessions, not synced to any server, and not shared with any third party.

Third-Party Services

Lotto Check does not include any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporters, or SDKs. The app makes no network connections.

Children's Privacy

The app is rated 17+ because of references to lottery games. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
