How to use

  1. Pick your game — Pick 3, Pick 4, or Pick 5
  2. Optional: turn on Fireball and choose your digit (0–9)
  3. Enter the winning draws (one per line, or comma-separated)
  4. Enter your played tickets (one per line, comma-separated, or pasted as a list)
  5. Tap Check My Tickets to see your wins

Match types

  • Straight — numbers match in exact order
  • Box — numbers match in any order
  • Fireball Straight / Box — same checks with the Fireball digit substituted into each position of a winning draw

FAQ

Does this app sell lottery tickets or facilitate gambling?

No. Lotto Check is a verification utility only. It does not sell tickets, conduct lotteries, process payments, or link to gambling sites.

Is my data saved or sent anywhere?

No. Everything you type is processed locally and is not stored when you close the app. The app makes no network connections.

Why is there a 2,000-ticket limit on the Your Tickets section?

To keep the app responsive. If you need to check more, run them in batches.
